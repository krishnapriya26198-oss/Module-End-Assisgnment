# Healthcare Data Analysis Using Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a healthcare dataset using **Microsoft Excel**. The analysis was performed using **PivotTables and charts** to identify patterns and relationships between patient characteristics, medical conditions, hospital information, and healthcare charges.

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used to summarize healthcare data and present the results through clear visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze the distribution of cancer history among smokers and non-smokers.
* Compare healthcare charges across different weight and diabetes categories.
* Compare average healthcare charges across hospital tiers and states.
* Explore relationships between age, BMI, HbA1C, and healthcare charges.
* Use appropriate charts to communicate the results clearly.

---

# 📈 Data Analysis & Visualizations

## 1. Cancer History by Smoking Status

A **Pie/Donut Chart** was used to analyze the distribution of cancer history among smokers and non-smokers.

### PivotTable Setup

* **Rows:** Smoker
* **Columns:** Cancer History
* **Values:** Count of Cancer History

This visualization helps compare the number of patients with and without a history of cancer based on smoking status.

---

## 2. Major Surgeries and Average HbA1C by Transplant History

The analysis compares patients with and without a history of transplant based on:

* Total number of major surgeries
* Average HbA1C

### PivotTable Setup

* **Rows:** History of Transplant
* **Values:** Sum of Major Surgeries
* **Values:** Average HbA1C

This analysis helps identify differences in surgical history and HbA1C levels between patients with different transplant histories.

---

## 3. Healthcare Charges by Weight and Diabetes Status

A **Clustered Column Chart** was created to compare healthcare charges across different weight statuses and diabetes statuses.

### PivotTable Setup

* **Rows:** Weight Status
* **Columns:** Diabetes Status
* **Values:** Sum of Healthcare Charges

### Chart Title

**Healthcare Charges by Weight and Diabetes Status**

This visualization allows healthcare charges to be compared between diabetic and non-diabetic patients across different weight categories.

---

## 4. Average Healthcare Charges by Hospital Tier and State

A **Clustered Column Chart** was used to compare the average healthcare charges for different hospital tiers across states.

### PivotTable Setup

* **Rows:** State
* **Columns:** Hospital Tier
* **Values:** Average Healthcare Charges

### Chart Title

**Average Healthcare Charges by Hospital Tier and State**

This chart makes it possible to compare average healthcare charges among different hospital tiers within each state.

---

## 5. Age and BMI Relationship

A **Scatter/Line Chart** was used to explore the relationship between patient age and BMI.

### PivotTable Setup

* **Rows:** Age
* **Values:** Average BMI

A trendline was used to help identify the overall relationship between age and BMI.

### Chart Title

**Age vs BMI**

## 6. Age and HbA1C Relationship
